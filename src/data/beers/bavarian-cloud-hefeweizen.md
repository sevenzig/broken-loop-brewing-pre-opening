# Bavarian Cloud Hefeweizen

Our homage to the classic German weissbier tradition, brewed with authentic German ingredients and techniques. This naturally cloudy wheat beer showcases the beautiful interplay between traditional Bavarian wheat malt and the distinctive character of German weissbier yeast.

## Brewing Notes

Brewed with 60% German wheat malt and 40% German Pilsner malt, following traditional Bavarian brewing methods. The signature banana and clove flavors come naturally from our authentic Weihenstephan yeast strain, fermented at carefully controlled temperatures to achieve the perfect balance.

- Light salads and fresh vegetables
