# Berry Bliss Sour

Our seasonal celebration of summer's finest berries, this kettle sour showcases
the perfect harmony between tart and sweet. We carefully balance three types of
berries to create a complex yet refreshing drinking experience.

## Fruit Forward Philosophy

Berry Bliss features over 2 pounds of fruit per barrel, using only the finest:

- **Blackberries**: Provide deep color and rich, jammy sweetness
- **Blueberries**: Add subtle tartness and beautiful purple hues
- **Raspberries**: Contribute bright acidity and aromatic intensity

All fruit is sourced from local farms within 50 miles of our brewery, ensuring
peak freshness and supporting our community.

## Food Pairings

- Fresh salads with goat cheese and vinaigrette
- Grilled chicken or pork
- Berry desserts and cheesecake
- Soft cheeses like brie or camembert
- Dark chocolate
