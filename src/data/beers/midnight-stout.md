# Midnight Stout - Darkness Awaits

A bold and complex imperial stout that embodies the mystery of the night. This
rich, full-bodied beer delivers layers of roasted malt complexity with subtle
bourbon barrel influences that warm the soul.

## Brewing Notes

Our grain bill features a foundation of 2-Row Pale malt with generous additions
of Chocolate malt and Roasted Barley for deep color and roasted flavors. Flaked
oats add a silky mouthfeel that makes this stout incredibly smooth.

Aged for 6 months in bourbon barrels, this stout develops complex vanilla and
oak characteristics that complement the roasted malt backbone. The extended
aging process creates a harmonious blend of flavors.
