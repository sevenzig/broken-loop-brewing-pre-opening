# Hazy Horizon IPA

Our flagship New England IPA represents everything we love about the hazy revolution. Brewed with a generous amount of wheat malt and flaked oats for that signature smooth, pillowy mouthfeel that defines the style.

## Brewing Notes

Unlike traditional West Coast IPAs, Hazy Horizon focuses on hop flavor and aroma rather than bitterness. We dry-hop this beer with over 3 pounds per barrel of premium hops, creating an intensely aromatic and flavorful experience without the harsh bite.

## Food Pairings

- Spicy Thai or Indian cuisine
- Fresh seafood and sushi
- Citrus-forward desserts
- Sharp cheeses like aged cheddar
