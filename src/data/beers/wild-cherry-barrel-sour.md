# Wild Cherry Barrel Sour

Our wild fermentation project showcases the beauty of controlled chaos. This complex sour ale spends over two years in red wine barrels with our house culture of wild yeasts and bacteria, then undergoes a final aging period with fresh Michigan cherries.

## Wild Fermentation Process

This beer begins its journey as a simple wort of pilsner and wheat malts, then is inoculated with our carefully cultivated mixed culture containing Brettanomyces, Lactobacillus, and Pediococcus. The wild fermentation creates complex esters and acids that develop over 18+ months.

## Cherry Addition

In the final six months, we add 2 pounds per gallon of fresh Michigan tart cherries, allowing the fruit to integrate naturally with the sour base. The cherries add brightness and color while complementing the existing acidity.
