# Golden Abbey Tripel

Our interpretation of the classic Belgian Tripel style, inspired by the great Trappist monasteries of Belgium. This deceptively strong ale combines complex Belgian yeast character with the finest European malts and hops, creating a beer that's both contemplative and celebratory.

## Brewing Notes

Brewed with traditional Belgian Pilsner malt and pure candi sugar, this tripel undergoes extended fermentation with authentic Belgian yeast. The result is a beer with remarkable depth and complexity that belies its clean, golden appearance.
