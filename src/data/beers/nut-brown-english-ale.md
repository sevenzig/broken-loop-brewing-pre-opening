# Nut Brown English Ale

A faithful recreation of the classic English brown ale style, showcasing the beauty of traditional English malts and hops. This malt-forward ale emphasizes the rich, complex flavors that made brown ales a pub favorite throughout England.

## Brewing Notes

Brewed exclusively with English ingredients, including Maris Otter pale malt and authentic English crystal and chocolate malts. The traditional English ale yeast produces subtle fruity esters that complement rather than compete with the rich malt character.
