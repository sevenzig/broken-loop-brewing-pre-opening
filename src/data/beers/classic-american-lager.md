# Classic American Lager

Our tribute to the great American lager tradition - a clean, honest beer that prioritizes drinkability and refreshment. Brewed with traditional American ingredients including corn and rice, this lager represents the craft brewery's take on America's most popular beer style.

## Brewing Notes

Brewed with 6-row barley malt and adjuncts of flaked rice and corn grits, following traditional American lager techniques. Extended cold conditioning creates the signature clean, crisp character that makes this beer incredibly drinkable.
