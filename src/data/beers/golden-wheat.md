# Golden Wheat - Sunshine in a Glass

A classic American wheat beer that's as refreshing as a summer breeze. This
light and approachable beer features a smooth wheat character with subtle spice
notes that make it perfect for any occasion.

## Brewing Notes

Crafted with a 50/50 blend of wheat and barley malts, this beer showcases the
classic wheat beer character while maintaining excellent drinkability. A touch
of Vienna malt adds depth and complexity to the grain bill.

Traditional German noble hops provide a gentle, floral bitterness that
complements rather than competes with the wheat character. The result is a
perfectly balanced, sessionable beer.

## Food Pairings

- Light salads and seafood
- Soft cheeses and pretzels
- Grilled chicken and vegetables
- Fruit tarts and lemon desserts
- Spicy Mexican cuisine
