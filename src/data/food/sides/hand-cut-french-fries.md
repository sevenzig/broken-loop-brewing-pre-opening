# Hand-Cut French Fries - Made Fresh Daily

These aren't frozen fries from a bag. We start with fresh russet potatoes and cut them by hand every morning, then double-fry them for the perfect texture.

## The Process

We use only the best russet potatoes, cut them fresh daily, and soak them to remove excess starch. Then we double-fry them: first at a lower temperature to cook them through, then at a higher temperature to create that perfect golden, crispy exterior.

## The Result

- **Crispy Exterior** - Golden brown and perfectly crunchy
- **Fluffy Interior** - Soft and tender on the inside
- **Sea Salt** - Lightly seasoned with coarse sea salt

## Why They're Special

The double-frying technique creates the perfect texture: crispy on the outside, fluffy on the inside. Every batch is made fresh, so you get the best fries possible.

## Perfect Pairing

These fries are the ideal companion to any of our burgers. They're also great on their own or with our house-made dipping sauces.
