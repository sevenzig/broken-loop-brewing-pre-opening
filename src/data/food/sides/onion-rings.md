# Beer-Battered Onion Rings - Crispy and Golden

Our thick-cut onion rings are dipped in our signature beer batter made with our own craft beer, then fried until golden and crispy.

## The Batter

We use our own craft beer in the batter, which adds a subtle flavor and creates an incredibly light, crispy coating. The batter is seasoned with our secret blend of spices for extra flavor.

## The Onions

We use only sweet onions, cut thick to ensure they stay tender inside while the outside gets perfectly crispy. Each ring is hand-dipped to ensure even coverage.

## The Result

- **Crispy Coating** - Light, golden, and perfectly crunchy
- **Tender Inside** - Sweet and soft onion that melts in your mouth
- **Perfect Size** - Thick enough to have substance, not too thick to be overwhelming

## Perfect Pairing

These onion rings are the perfect side for any burger, or great on their own with our house-made dipping sauces.
