# Bacon Cheeseburger - A Classic Done Right

Everything you love about our classic burger, elevated with crispy bacon and melted cheddar cheese.

## The Perfect Combination

Our hand-formed beef patty is topped with thick-cut, crispy bacon and melted sharp cheddar cheese. The combination of smoky bacon, creamy cheese, and juicy beef creates the ultimate burger experience.

## The Build

- **Beef Patty** - 6 oz hand-formed patty, cooked to order
- **Bacon** - Thick-cut, crispy strips
- **Cheese** - Sharp cheddar, melted to perfection
- **Bun** - Toasted brioche bun, buttered and grilled
- **Toppings** - Fresh lettuce, ripe tomato, red onion, and house-made pickles
- **Sauce** - Our special sauce

## Comes With

Choice of one side: hand-cut fries, roasted Brussels sprouts, coleslaw, or onion rings.

## Why It's Special

The crispy bacon adds a smoky, salty crunch that perfectly complements the juicy beef and creamy cheese. It's a flavor combination that never gets old.
