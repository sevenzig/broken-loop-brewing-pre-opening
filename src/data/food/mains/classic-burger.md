# Classic Burger - The Perfect Burger

Our signature burger starts with a hand-formed, never-frozen beef patty cooked to perfection and served on a toasted brioche bun.

## The Patty

We use fresh, premium ground beef that's hand-formed daily. Each patty is cooked to your preference, ensuring a juicy, flavorful burger every time.

## The Build

- **Beef Patty** - 6 oz hand-formed patty, cooked to order
- **Bun** - Toasted brioche bun, buttered and grilled
- **Toppings** - Fresh lettuce, ripe tomato, red onion, and house-made pickles
- **Sauce** - Our special sauce with a perfect balance of tangy and creamy

## Comes With

Choice of one side: hand-cut fries, roasted Brussels sprouts, coleslaw, or onion rings.

## Customization

Add cheese, bacon, or any of our premium toppings. We're happy to make it your way!
