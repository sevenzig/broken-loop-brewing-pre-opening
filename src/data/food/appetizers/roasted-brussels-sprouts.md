# Roasted Brussels Sprouts - A Flavorful Favorite

Forget everything you thought you knew about Brussels sprouts. These crispy, caramelized sprouts will convert even the biggest skeptics.

## The Preparation

We start with fresh Brussels sprouts, halved and tossed with olive oil, garlic, and seasonings. They're roasted at high heat until the edges are perfectly crispy and golden brown, while the centers remain tender.

## The Finishing Touch

- **Balsamic Glaze** - A sweet and tangy reduction that adds depth
- **Crispy Bacon** - Smoky, salty bits that complement the sprouts
- **Parmesan Cheese** - Freshly grated for a nutty finish

## Why They're Special

The high-heat roasting process caramelizes the natural sugars in the Brussels sprouts, creating a sweet, nutty flavor that pairs perfectly with the tangy balsamic and salty bacon. It's a perfect balance of textures and flavors.

## Perfect For

- Sharing as an appetizer
- A lighter side option
- Vegetarian diners (when ordered without bacon)
