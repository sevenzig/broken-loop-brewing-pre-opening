# Trivia Thursdays at Broken Loop Brewing

Join us every Thursday night for an exciting trivia challenge that will test
your knowledge across a variety of topics! Whether you're a trivia master or
just looking for a fun night out, our weekly trivia nights offer something for
everyone.

## What to Expect

Our trivia nights feature multiple rounds covering everything from pop culture
and sports to history and science. Teams of up to 6 people compete for fantastic
prizes, including brewery gift cards, branded merchandise, and even growler
fills of our signature beers.

## Prizes & Rewards

- **1st Place**: $50 brewery gift card + branded merchandise
- **2nd Place**: $25 brewery gift card
- **3rd Place**: Brewery t-shirts for the team
- **Participation**: 10% off food orders for all trivia participants

## How It Works

- Teams can have up to 6 members
- Registration starts at 6:30 PM
- Trivia begins promptly at 7:00 PM
- 5 rounds of questions with varying point values
- Food and drinks available throughout the event

Come early to grab a table and enjoy our rotating selection of craft beers and
delicious food menu. Our trivia host keeps the energy high and the questions
challenging but fair.

**No reservations required** - just show up and have fun!
