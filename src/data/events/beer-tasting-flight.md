# IPA Tasting Flight Experience

Dive deep into the world of India Pale Ales with our expert-led tasting
experience! Join Head Brewer Sarah for an educational and delicious journey
through five carefully selected IPAs, each showcasing different hop profiles and
brewing techniques.

## What's Included

- **5 beer samples** (4 oz each) featuring different IPA styles
- **Tasting notes** and flavor profile cards for each beer
- **Expert guidance** from our Head Brewer Sarah
- **Food pairings** - small bites designed to complement each beer
- **Take-home materials** including hop variety guides and brewing tips

## Featured IPAs

This month's flight explores the spectrum of hop flavors:

1. **West Coast IPA** - Citrus and pine forward
2. **New England IPA** - Tropical and juicy
3. **Session IPA** - Light and approachable
4. **Double IPA** - Bold and intense
5. **Specialty IPA** - Unique seasonal variant

## Educational Components

- **Hop varieties** and their flavor contributions
- **Brewing techniques** that affect IPA character
- **Proper tasting methods** to identify flavors and aromas
- **Food pairing principles** for hoppy beers
- **Home brewing tips** for IPA enthusiasts

## Who Should Attend

Perfect for:

- Beer enthusiasts wanting to expand their palate
- Home brewers looking to understand IPA styles
- Anyone curious about the craft behind hoppy beers
- Date night with a educational twist
- Groups celebrating special occasions
