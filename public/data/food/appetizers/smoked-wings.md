# Smoked Wings - Not Your Average Wings

Forget everything you know about chicken wings. Our jumbo wings are dry-rubbed
and smoked low and slow until they develop that perfect crispy skin while
staying juicy inside.

## Sauce Options

- **Classic Buffalo** - Traditional wing sauce with a kick
- **BBQ Glaze** - Our signature sweet and tangy BBQ sauce
- **Honey Garlic** - Sweet honey balanced with roasted garlic
- **Dry Rub** - Just the way they come off the smoker

## Why They're Special

Unlike traditional fried wings, our smoking process renders the fat slowly,
creating incredibly crispy skin without any oil. The result is a wing that's
both healthier and more flavorful.

## Serving

Comes with celery sticks, carrots, and house-made ranch or blue cheese dressing.
