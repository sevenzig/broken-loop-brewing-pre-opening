# Bourbon Baked Beans - A BBQ Classic with a Twist

These aren't your typical baked beans. We slow-cook navy beans with a splash of
bourbon and chunks of our smoked burnt ends for an unforgettable side dish.

## The Secret Ingredients

- **Bourbon** - Adds depth and complexity
- **Molasses** - For that rich, sweet flavor
- **Burnt ends** - Cubes of our smoked brisket for texture
- **Brown sugar** - Balances the acidity

## The Process

We start by soaking the beans overnight, then slow-cook them with our special
blend of seasonings. The bourbon cooks off, leaving behind its distinctive
flavor while the burnt ends add smoky meat in every bite.

## Perfect Pairing

These beans are the ideal companion to any of our smoked meats, especially the
brisket or pulled pork. The sweet and savory flavors complement the smokiness
perfectly.

## Made Fresh Daily

We make these beans fresh every morning in small batches to ensure maximum
flavor and consistency.
