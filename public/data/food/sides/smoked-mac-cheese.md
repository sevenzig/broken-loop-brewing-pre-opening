# Smoked Mac & Cheese - Not Your Average Side

This isn't the mac and cheese from a box. We make our three-cheese sauce from
scratch, then finish it in the smoker for that subtle hint of smoke.

## The Cheese Blend

We use a combination of sharp cheddar for flavor, gruyere for creaminess, and
cream cheese to bind it all together. The result is incredibly rich and
satisfying.

## The Smoking Process

After baking until bubbly, we transfer the mac and cheese to the smoker for the
final 15 minutes. This adds a subtle smokiness that pairs perfectly with our
BBQ.

## Toppings

- **Bacon bits** - Crispy smoked bacon
- **Breadcrumbs** - House-made and toasted
- **Extra cheese** - Because you can never have too much

## Why It's Special

The combination of creamy cheese sauce and that hint of smoke makes this the
perfect complement to any of our smoked meats. It's comfort food elevated to the
next level.
