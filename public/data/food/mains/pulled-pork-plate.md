# Pulled Pork Plate - Comfort Food at Its Best

Our Boston butt pork shoulder is rubbed with our secret blend of spices and
smoked for 12 hours until it pulls apart effortlessly.

## The Process

We take a whole pork shoulder, massage it with our signature dry rub, and let it
absorb those flavors overnight. Then it goes into the smoker at 225°F where it
slowly transforms into something magical.

## What You Get

- **8 oz pulled pork** - Hand-pulled fresh from the smoker
- **Two sides** - Choose any two from our selection
- **Cornbread** - Sweet and crumbly, baked fresh daily
- **Sauce** - Our tangy vinegar-based sauce on the side

## Side Options

- Creamy coleslaw
- Baked beans with burnt ends
- Mac and cheese
- Hand-cut fries
- Potato salad
- Collard greens

## The Perfect Combination

Try it with coleslaw and baked beans for the classic BBQ experience, or go with
mac and cheese and fries if you're feeling indulgent.
