# Smoked Brisket Sandwich - The King of Sandwiches

Our signature brisket, smoked for 14 hours until it's tender enough to cut with
a fork, piled high on a toasted brioche bun.

## The Brisket

We start with prime grade beef brisket, season it with our secret rub, and smoke
it low and slow at 225°F until it reaches the perfect internal temperature. The
result is meat so tender it falls apart at the touch of a fork.

## The Build

- **Meat** - 8 oz of sliced smoked brisket
- **Bun** - Toasted brioche bun
- **Toppings** - House-made pickles and crispy red onions
- **Sauce** - Our signature BBQ sauce served on the side

## Comes With

Choice of one side: coleslaw, baked beans, mac and cheese, fries, or potato
salad.

## Pro Tip

For the full experience, dip each bite in our tangy BBQ sauce, but the brisket
is so flavorful it's amazing on its own too.
