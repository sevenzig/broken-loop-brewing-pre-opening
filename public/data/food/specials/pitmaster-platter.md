# Pitmaster's Platter - The Ultimate BBQ Experience

Our most popular item and the crown jewel of our menu. This generous platter
showcases the best of our pit-smoked meats, each prepared with our signature
rubs and slow-smoked to perfection over hickory wood.

## What's Included

- **Brisket** - 14-hour smoked, tender and juicy with a perfect bark
- **Pulled Pork** - Boston butt smoked for 12 hours, hand-pulled fresh
- **Baby Back Ribs** - Half rack of our award-winning ribs
- **House Sausage** - Made fresh daily with our secret blend of spices
- **Two Sides** - Choose from any of our homemade sides

## The Process

Each meat is seasoned with our proprietary rub blend and smoked low and slow in
our custom-built offset smoker. We use only hickory wood for that authentic
smokehouse flavor that keeps people coming back.

## Perfect For

- First-time visitors who want to try everything
- Sharing between 2-3 people
- Special occasions and celebrations
- Anyone serious about great BBQ
