## Overall Impression

A refreshing, thirst-quenching, sessionable, everyday beer. Prominent flavors are a pleasant grainy-sweet malt character and a spicy, floral Saaz hop character. Clean fermentation profile, with low diacetyl acceptable. Low to moderate bitterness.

## Aroma

Medium-low to medium-high rich, complex maltiness has a pleasant, grainy-sweet character. Low to moderately-high spicy, floral, or herbal hop character. Clean lager character, with low diacetyl acceptable. Low fruity esters are acceptable, but need not be present.

## Appearance

Gold to deep gold color. Brilliant to very clear clarity. Dense, long-lasting, creamy white head.

## Flavor

Rich, complex, grainy-sweet malt flavor has a rounded character that often suggests caramel. Low to moderate-low spicy, floral, or herbal hop flavor. Prominent hop flavor is not appropriate. Low hop bitterness. Low diacetyl is acceptable. Low to moderate fruity esters and alcohol are acceptable, but need not be present. Medium-dry to dry crisp finish. Clean fermentation profile is preferred, but low esters and diacetyl are acceptable.

## Mouthfeel

Medium-light to medium body. Moderate carbonation.

## History

Commonly associated with Pilsner Urquell, which was first brewed in Plzeň (Pilsen), Bohemia, in 1842 by Josef Groll, a Bavarian brewer. The name Pilsner is derived from the city of Plzeň. The original Pilsner was brewed with English malting techniques, Moravian barley, Saaz hops, and soft water. This beer was revolutionary for its time – the first golden lager, brewed with the newly developed indirect heating and Bavarian lagering techniques, along with the recently invented steam engine and refrigeration.

## Comments

Generally a group of pivo with a rich, complex, malty flavor and a spicy, floral Saaz hop character. Most examples are of moderate strength. The Czech name of this style is světlé výčepní pivo.
