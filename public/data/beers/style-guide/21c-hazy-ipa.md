## Overall Impression

An American IPA with intense fruit flavors and aromas, a soft body, smooth mouthfeel, and often opaque with substantial haze. Less perceived bitterness than traditional IPAs but always massively hop-forward. This style emphasizes late hopping, especially dry-hopping, with hops with tropical fruit and citrus characteristics. Sometimes called New England IPA or NEIPA.

## Aroma

Intense hop aroma, with stone fruit, tropical, and citrus aspects. Many examples have a creamy, vanilla, or coconut aroma. Fruity-estery aroma is also common. Little to no perceived malt. Neutral to fruity fermentation character. Alcohol may be noticeable in stronger examples.

## Appearance

Color ranging from straw to deep gold, sometimes with orange hues. Very hazy to completely opaque. Creamy, dense, white to off-white head with good retention.

## Flavor

High to very high hop flavor, same descriptors as aroma. Low to medium-low perceived bitterness, often masked by the full body and soft, creamy mouthfeel. Fruity-estery flavor is common. Low to medium malt flavor, generally neutral. Clean fermentation profile, but fruity-estery notes are acceptable. Alcohol may be noticeable in stronger examples. Medium-dry to dry finish. Not sweet, although fruit flavors and lower bitterness may give that impression.

## Mouthfeel

Medium to medium-full body. Smooth. Little to no astringency, although alcohol warming is acceptable in stronger examples. Medium to medium-high carbonation.

## History

A modern craft beer style originating in the New England region of the United States. Alchemist Heady Topper is believed to be the original inspiration for many commercial examples. Popularized by Tree House, Trillium, The Alchemist, and other New England breweries. The style continues to evolve, including a trend towards lower bitterness and using lactose and fruit additions.

## Comments

The haze is a byproduct of dry-hopping and the use of oats or wheat, but is not a requirement. Many examples have a creamy mouthfeel, fruity flavor, and aroma. Heavy examples with lactose or fruit additions should be entered as a Specialty IPA. The use of lactose would create a sweeter beer and is not traditional for the style; it should be used judiciously if at all. The term "Juicy" is sometimes used to describe this style, referring to the fruity hop character.

## Vital Statistics

- **OG:** 1.060 – 1.085
- **FG:** 1.010 – 1.015  
- **ABV:** 6.0 – 9.0%
- **IBUs:** 25 – 60
- **SRM:** 3 – 7

## Commercial Examples

**American:** The Alchemist Heady Topper, Tree House Julius, Trillium Congress Street, Hill Farmstead Susan, Tired Hands HopHands, WeldWerks Juicy Bits
