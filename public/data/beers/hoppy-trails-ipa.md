# Hoppy Trails IPA - A Journey Through Flavor

Our flagship IPA is a bold expression of American hop character, featuring a
carefully curated blend of Pacific Northwest hops that deliver bright citrus and
resinous pine flavors. This West Coast-style IPA showcases the perfect balance
between malt sweetness and hop bitterness.

## Brewing Notes

Brewed with premium 2-Row Pale malt as the base, we add Munich malt for depth
and Crystal 60L for a touch of caramel sweetness that balances the hop
intensity. The grist is kept simple to let the hops shine through.

Our hop schedule features three additions: Cascade provides the floral
foundation, Centennial brings the citrus punch, and Simcoe delivers those piney,
resinous notes that IPA lovers crave. We dry hop for maximum aroma impact.
