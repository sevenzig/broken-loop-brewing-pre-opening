# Midnight Barrel Imperial Stout

Our most ambitious brewing project - a massive imperial stout that spends 18 months slumbering in hand-selected bourbon barrels. This limited release represents the pinnacle of our barrel aging program, with only 200 bottles produced annually.

## Brewing Notes

This beer begins as a 10% ABV imperial stout base, carefully crafted with six different malts to create layers of chocolate, coffee, and caramel flavors. After primary fermentation, it's transferred to bourbon barrels where it develops its signature complexity over 18 months of patient aging.

## Barrel Selection

Each barrel is carefully selected from premium bourbon distilleries, contributing unique character from previous bourbon aging. The extended aging process allows the beer to extract vanilla, oak tannins, and residual bourbon flavors while smoothing the high alcohol content.
