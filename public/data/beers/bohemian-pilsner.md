# Bohemian Pilsner

Our tribute to the world's first golden lager, created in 1842 in Plzeň, Czech Republic. This authentic interpretation showcases the elegance and balance that made pilsner the world's most popular beer style.

## Brewing Notes

We honor centuries of Czech brewing tradition with authentic techniques:

- **Decoction Mashing**: Traditional triple decoction for enhanced malt character
- **Floor Malted Grains**: Bohemian Pilsner malt for authentic flavor
- **Noble Hops**: 100% Czech Saaz hops from the Žatec region
- **Extended Lagering**: 6 weeks of cold conditioning for smoothness
