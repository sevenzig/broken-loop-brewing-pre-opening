# Live Music Saturday: The Brew Notes

Get ready for an unforgettable evening of live music at Broken Loop Brewing!
This Saturday, we're thrilled to host **The Brew Notes**, a local acoustic folk
rock trio known for their soulful harmonies and engaging stage presence.

## About The Brew Notes

The Brew Notes have been captivating audiences throughout the region with their
unique blend of folk, rock, and indie sounds. Featuring acoustic guitar, violin,
and rich vocal harmonies, they create an intimate musical experience that pairs
perfectly with our craft beer atmosphere.

## Event Details

- **Performance Time**: 8:00 PM - 11:00 PM
- **Genre**: Acoustic Folk Rock
- **Seating**: First come, first served
- **Cover**: No cover charge
- **Age**: 21+ after 9:00 PM, all ages welcome before

## What to Expect

- **Original compositions** and creative covers of popular songs
- **Interactive performance** - feel free to make requests!
- **Full bar service** featuring our rotating tap selection
- **Food menu** available throughout the performance
- **Relaxed atmosphere** perfect for date nights or hanging with friends

## Food & Drink Specials

During live music events, we offer:

- Happy hour prices on select beers until 9:00 PM
- Live music food pairing menu
- Specialty cocktails inspired by the performing artists

## Getting Here

We recommend arriving early to secure good seating. Our taproom fills up quickly
during live music events! Parking is available in our lot and on nearby streets.

**Support local music and local beer - we'll see you Saturday night!**
